Better Than Us, or Replacing Us?

Could AI write a better news story? Will it mean fewer jobs for journalists? Americans weighed in on both questions in 2024. Employment projections and a survey of U.S. businesses show where jobs may change and how AI is already being used.

Which jobs might shrink?

Pew asked what AI might do to jobs over 20 years. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) projects how overall employment may change from 2025 to 2035, taking many factors into account.

How the jobs match
  • Factory workers are represented by production jobs within manufacturing (2022 NAICS 31–33; BLS occupation 51-0000).
  • Teachers cover preschool to high school, including special education. Truck drivers combine heavy and light truck drivers. Doctors combine physicians and surgeons.
  • Mental health therapists are represented by marriage and family therapists and by substance abuse, behavioral disorder, and mental health counselors.
  • BLS groups software engineers under software developers. Its journalist group includes news analysts and reporters, and its musician group includes singers.
